Size ​

Applies different sizes to buttons.

<button class="c-button c-button--size-sm">
	Small
</button>

<button class="c-button c-button--size-md">
	Small
</button>

<button class="c-button c-button--size-lg">
	Small
</button>

</button>

</button>
